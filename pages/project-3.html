Lighthouse on the Marsh

At Lighthouse on the Marsh, I helped lead a Lottery-funded programme called Generations Connect, which aimed to bridge the gap between disconnected youth and elderly communities across the Romney Marsh. As part of this programme, I designed print-ready, engaging marketing materials, conducted hyperlocal, low-budget ad campaigns, and conducted both one-to-one and group sessions in IT and digital creative skills. The programme was a major success, getting the attention of local media and being granted a funding extension by the National Lottery Community Fund.

Details

  • Role: Hyperlocal, low-budget marketing, one-to-one IT training
