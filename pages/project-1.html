Dover Marina Hotel & Spa

Being part of a shortlisted creative team for a landmark like the Hilton Tapestry Collection Dover Marina Hotel & Spa requires more than just a good eye; it requires a high degree of radical adaptability.

My role centered on upholding the visual prestige of a luxury destination while balancing the needs of third-party partners, such as Nangai Boutique, who utilize the hotel’s high-end aesthetic as a backdrop.

Whether I'm snapping wide-angle shots of renovated rooms, assisting with mini-campaigns at the Churchill Bar & Lounge or working with unconventional lighting, any time spent at the Dover Marina Hotel is an opportunity to benchmark my skills.
