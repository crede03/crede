AI Bollocks

What did this side-project accomplish? Honestly, who knows.

In 2023, AI was still in its infancy - ChatGPT was nothing more than a toy, Will Smith couldn't eat spaghetti, and Copilot wasn't baked into literally every Microsoft product.

AI Bollocks was my foray into early AI adoption, creating parody videos using RVC for voice and ModelScope for video generation. It took off almost immediately. My videos of shamed celebrities and politicians like Philip Schofield and Theresa May took off on TikTok, and eventually found their way onto various lazy Instagram meme pages - and Reddit.

My biggest hit by far was AI-Generated Top Gear. Ironically, not much of this was actually AI-generated. Sure, the video was, but a good amount of the voice lines used me as the source, the scripts were written by me, and later episodes eventually morphed into machinima-style videos utilising games like BeamNG to break up the mushy, 144x144 video. A cover of Jeremy Clarkson eventually made it to DriveTribe's Facebook.

AI Bollocks still has an impact today. My biggest regret was cloning Keir Starmer's voice and uploading it for anyone to use, causing an influx of fake videos with his voice. It also serves a time capsule for early AI, and a reminder of how good, and scary, this medium has become. That's exactly the reason why I stopped - it's not funny when it's lifelike.

If, miraculously, Google actually ranks this portfolio and you're a fan of the channel, please rest assured - I'm not dead. I will come back at some point. I'm waiting for the right time.
